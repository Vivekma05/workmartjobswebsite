1. What WorkMart is

WorkMart is a technology platform that connects two kinds of users: Providers (people or businesses who post gig jobs) and Workers (people who apply for and perform those jobs). WorkMart also provides an escrow facility, through a licensed payment partner, so that a Provider's payment is held securely and released to the Worker when the job is confirmed complete.

WorkMart is a facilitator and marketplace only. WorkMart is not the employer of any Worker, is not a party to the service contract between a Provider and a Worker, and does not itself perform any gig work. Workers are independent service providers, not employees or agents of WorkMart.

4. Posting and accepting jobs

  • A Provider posts a job with a description, category, date/time, location and budget, and funds the total amount (budget + platform fee) into escrow.
  • Workers may apply. The Provider selects one Worker; other applicants are automatically declined for that job.
  • A Worker may be accepted for a job only if they are not already committed to another job whose time overlaps (including a travel buffer). This prevents double-booking.
  • Both parties agree to deal honestly, communicate through the app, and complete the job as described.

5. Verification codes

To confirm a job genuinely happened, WorkMart uses two codes: a start code the Provider shares with the Worker on arrival, and a completion code the Worker shares with the Provider once the work and photo proof are submitted. The Provider must enter the completion code before payment is released. You agree not to share these codes except as intended by the flow, or to use them to misrepresent whether work was done.

6. Payments, escrow & payouts

  • WorkMart charges a platform fee as described on our Pricing page. The fee is shown to the Provider before payment.
  • When a Provider confirms completion (after entering the completion code), the Worker's earning is released to their provided payout method (e.g. UPI).
  • Refunds and cancellations are governed by our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
  • You are responsible for your own taxes on income earned or payments made through the Platform.

9. Disputes

WorkMart will review the evidence (chat, photos, location, codes) and decide a fair outcome — release to the Worker, refund to the Provider, or a split. WorkMart's dispute decision, made in good faith, is final for the purposes of releasing or refunding the escrowed amount. This does not affect your legal rights.
