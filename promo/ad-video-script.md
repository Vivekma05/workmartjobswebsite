# WorkMart 30-second ad — shoot it on your phone today

No camera crew needed. Every shot is a **screen recording of the real app**
on a test account — authentic product footage beats stock video, and it
doubles as Play Store preview material later.

## Setup (10 min)

1. Two test accounts on two phones: **Provider** + **Worker** (or one phone,
   sign out/in between shots — slower but works).
2. Post one real ₹100 test job (cleaning), fund it, apply, accept.
3. Phone: Do-Not-Disturb ON, brightness full, dark mode OFF (daylight
   footage reads better), charge 100%.
4. Screen recorder: Android built-in (swipe down → Screen record), 1080p.

## Shots (record each 2–3x, keep the best take)

| # | Sec | What to record | On-screen caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–4 | Feed scrolling, tap a job with FUNDED badge | "Real jobs near you. Money already locked in." |
| 2 | 4–10 | Provider: post-job → budget → **count-up total + FREE fee row** → Pay screen → success confetti | "Post a job. Zero platform fee. Escrow protects every rupee." |
| 3 | 10–16 | Worker: transit map with moving dot → mark complete → OTP screen | "Watch them arrive live. Codes prove the work happened." |
| 4 | 16–22 | Provider: confirm → earnings screen counting up → tip sheet → tip success | "One tap releases pay. Tips go 100% to the worker." |
| 5 | 22–30 | Review screen → 5-star-style verdict → confetti → applicant Gold tier badge close-up | "Gold workers. Verified people. **WorkMart — Kaam pakka, payment pakka.**" |

Hindi voiceover option (same timings): "Asli kaam, paise surakshit. WorkMart — kaam pakka, payment pakka."

## Edit in CapCut (free, 30 min)

1. New project → 9:16 (1080×1920) → import the 5 clips in order.
2. Trim to the seconds above; speed 1.1x on scrolling shots.
3. Text → Auto captions → style: bold, white with black stroke, bottom third.
4. Audio → Music → search "upbeat corporate royalty-free" (or record the
   Hindi line as voiceover — voiceover converts better for local services).
5. Transitions: 0.2s "Camera push" between shots only. Export 1080p/30fps.
6. Make a 1:1 copy (crop) for feed posts and a 16:9 copy for YouTube/Play.

## Where it goes (the actual strategy)

- **WhatsApp Status + local groups** (housing societies, area groups) — this
  is where India hires cleaners and painters. Post the poster + video together.
- **Referral loop pays for distribution**: every new user from the ad carries
  a ₹50 code — the ad keeps working after the view.
- **Play listing**: the 16:9 cut becomes your promo video slot; the feature
  graphic (`feature-graphic.svg` → export PNG 1024×500) is required anyway.
- **Testers first**: send the video to your 12+ testers and ask them to
  forward it to 2 local groups each. That is 24+ warm placements for free.
