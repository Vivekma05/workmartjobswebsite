Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: 30 July 2026

You can permanently delete your WorkMart account and personal data at any time, directly from the app. This page explains how, what gets deleted, what we're required to keep, and how to request deletion if you no longer have the app installed.

1. Delete in the app (self-serve, immediate)

  1. Open WorkMart and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away — no waiting period, no support ticket needed.

If you have money currently held in escrow — a job you posted that's funded, or a job you're actively working on — you'll be asked to finish, cancel, or resolve it first. This protects the other party in that job from having money stranded mid-transaction; it's the only thing that blocks deletion.

2. What gets deleted

Regardless of your account's history, the following are removed immediately and irreversibly:

  • Your KYC documents and verification images
  • Your saved jobs, favourited workers, and push-notification tokens
  • Your notification history
  • Uploaded files — profile photo, KYC images, job/completion photos you submitted
  • The text of messages you sent (the content is replaced with "[deleted]"; the conversation itself isn't wiped from the other participant's history, so their side of a chat isn't corrupted by your deletion)

3. What we're required to keep, and why

If your account has no financial history (no jobs posted, no payments, no payouts), it is deleted completely and permanently — nothing about you remains.

If your account does have financial history — you posted a job, made a payment, or received a payout — Indian tax and financial record-keeping rules require the underlying transaction ledger to be retained. In that case, we:

  • Replace your name with "Deleted user" and permanently erase your email, phone number, avatar, bio, skills, and saved location from our systems;
  • Disable the account entirely so it can never be signed into or recovered;
  • Keep only the anonymised transaction records themselves (job/payment/payout amounts and dates, with no name, contact detail, or profile attached to them) — the same way a bank statement keeps a transaction after you close an account.

No one at WorkMart can use these anonymised records to identify you afterward.

4. No app installed? Request deletion by email

If you'd rather not reinstall the app, email workmartjobs@gmail.com from the address on your account (or include your registered phone number) with the subject "Delete my account." We'll verify it's really you and complete the same deletion described above, and confirm once it's done.

In short: delete anytime from Settings in the app — it's instant. If you have no financial history, everything about you is erased. If you do, we anonymise your personal details but keep the transaction ledger itself, as Indian financial record-keeping rules require. No app? Email us and we'll do it for you.
