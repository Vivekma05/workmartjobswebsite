The process

How it works

A simple, protected flow from booking a service to completion.

1

Request & fund a service

A customer describes the service they need — the task, date, time and budget — then pays the amount into escrow through our licensed payment partner. The money is held safely; it is not sent to anyone yet.

2

Get matched & served

Nearby verified professionals offer to help. The customer picks one. The professional arrives, enters the start code to begin, completes the service and submits photo proof of the finished work.

3

Confirm & release payment

The customer verifies the completion code and confirms the service is done. WorkMart releases the escrowed amount to the professional. Both sides leave a review.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is my money safe?

Yes. Your payment goes into secure escrow via Razorpay when you book — not to the worker. It is released only after you enter the worker's completion code and confirm the work. If the job doesn't happen, the money comes back to you. See our refund policy.

What does WorkMart cost?

Browsing and joining are free, forever. Providers currently pay ₹0 platform fee under our launch offer — only the service budget. Workers keep 100% of every budget plus 100% of tips. Full details on the pricing page.

Do I need KYC verification?

Yes — to post jobs or earn money. Upload a document and a selfie in the app; review usually finishes within 24 hours. It is how both sides know the other person is real.

How and when do workers get paid?

When the provider confirms completion, earnings go to the worker's verified bank account (tips included). Bank verification takes 24–48 hours one time, at signup.

What if something goes wrong?

Raise a dispute in the app before confirming completion. Our team reviews the chat, photos, location and codes, and decides fairly — release, refund, or split. After confirmation, payments are final.

Which cities does WorkMart serve?

WorkMart works anywhere in India. Job density varies by area — turn on job alerts in the app and we'll notify you the moment work appears near you.
