FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my money safe?
Yes. When you hire, you pay into escrow through Razorpay — the money doesn’t go to the professional yet. It’s released only after they give you the completion code and you confirm the work. If the job doesn’t happen, you’re refunded under our refund policy.
How much does WorkMart cost?
Posting a job is free, and right now the platform fee is ₹0 too — you pay only the job budget, and only when you hire. Professionals join free and keep 100% of the budget and tips. See pricing & fees.
Do I need KYC verification?
Yes — to post jobs or to earn. Upload an ID document and a selfie in the app; our team usually reviews it within 24 hours. It’s how both sides know the other person is real.
How do professionals get paid?
Once the customer confirms the work, the payment is released to the professional’s verified bank account, along with any tip. Bank details are verified once, when you set up payouts.
What if something goes wrong?
Raise a dispute in the app before confirming the work. Our team reviews the chat, photos, location and codes, and decides fairly — release, refund or split. Once you confirm, the payment is final.
Where is WorkMart available?
Anywhere in India. How many jobs you’ll see depends on your area — turn on job alerts in the app and we’ll tell you the moment work is posted near you.
