How it works

A simple, protected flow from booking a service to completion.

1

Request & fund a service

A customer describes the service they need — the task, date, time and budget — then pays the amount into escrow through our licensed payment partner. The money is held safely; it is not sent to anyone yet.

2

Get matched & served

Nearby verified professionals offer to help. The customer picks one. The professional arrives, enters the start code to begin, completes the service and submits photo proof of the finished work.

3

Confirm & release payment

The customer verifies the completion code and confirms the service is done. WorkMart releases the escrowed amount to the professional. Both sides leave a review.

Simple, fair pricing

Customers pay only for the service they book. WorkMart adds a small service fee (a percentage of the booking) — that is our only source of revenue. Professionals join and offer their services for free and keep what they earn; WorkMart never charges a professional to sign up or to be matched. All payments are processed by a licensed payment gateway and held in escrow until the service is complete. See full details on our pricing page.
