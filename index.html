How it works

A simple, protected flow from posting a job to getting paid.

1

Post & fund a job

A provider posts a job with a date, time and budget, then pays the amount into escrow through our payment partner. The money is held safely — not sent to anyone yet.

2

Get matched & do the work

Nearby verified workers apply. The provider accepts one. The worker arrives, enters the start code to begin, completes the work and submits photo proof.

3

Confirm & release payment

The provider verifies the completion code and confirms the job. WorkMart releases the escrow to the worker's UPI. Everyone leaves a review.
