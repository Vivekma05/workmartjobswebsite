Pricing & Fees

Last updated: 9 September 2026

WorkMart is free to download and free to browse. For a limited period, our platform fee is ₹0 — providers pay only the job budget, and workers receive every rupee of it. When the offer ends, the standard fee below applies, always shown before you pay.
Launch offer: ₹0 platform fee. Posting is free, and when you hire you pay only the job budget — no added charges while the offer lasts. The app always shows the exact total before any payment is made.

Platform fee

Right now: ₹0 on every job. Our standard fee — 2% of the job budget, minimum ₹20 per job — is currently waived. It is added on top of the budget and collected with the escrow amount only when the offer is not active.

What the worker receives

The worker receives the full job budget (e.g. ₹500, ₹1,000, ₹2,000 in the examples above). The platform fee — when active — is the Provider's separate charge for using WorkMart's matching, escrow and safety features, and is never deducted from a worker's earnings.

Tips

After a job is paid out, providers can send the worker a tip (₹10–₹2,000) straight from the job page. 100% of every tip goes to the worker. Tips are voluntary, final and non-refundable.

Payment methods

Providers pay securely through our payment partner (Razorpay), which supports UPI, debit/credit cards and net banking. Worker payouts go directly to the worker's verified bank account.

Escrow & when money moves

  • Posting a job is free. The Provider pays the budget + platform fee (currently ₹0) into escrow when they hire a worker.
  • The money is held safely — not sent to the worker yet.
  • It is released to the worker only after the Provider confirms the job is complete (using the completion code).
  • If the job is cancelled before work is done, the Provider is refunded per our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Taxes

Prices shown in the app are inclusive of applicable amounts unless stated otherwise. Users are responsible for their own tax obligations on income earned or payments made through the Platform.

No hidden charges. The exact fee (currently ₹0) and total are shown to the Provider on the job screen before any payment is made.
