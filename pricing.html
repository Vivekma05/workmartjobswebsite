Pricing & Fees

Last updated: 21 July 2026

WorkMart is free to download and free to browse. We charge a small, transparent platform fee to the Provider on top of the job budget — always shown before you pay. Workers receive their full job budget; the platform fee is never deducted from a worker's earnings.

Platform fee

The platform fee is 2% of the job budget, with a minimum of ₹20 per job. It is added on top of the budget and collected together with the escrow amount when the Provider funds a job.

What the worker receives

The worker receives the full job budget (e.g. ₹500, ₹1,000, ₹2,000 in the examples above). The platform fee is the Provider's separate charge for using WorkMart's matching, escrow and safety features.

Payment methods

Providers pay securely through our payment partner (Razorpay), which supports UPI, debit/credit cards and net banking. Worker payouts are made to the worker's provided UPI ID.

Escrow & when money moves

  • The Provider pays the budget + platform fee into escrow when posting a funded job.
  • The money is held safely — not sent to the worker yet.
  • It is released to the worker only after the Provider confirms the job is complete (using the completion code).
  • If the job is cancelled before work is done, the Provider is refunded per our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Taxes

Prices shown in the app are inclusive of applicable amounts unless stated otherwise. Users are responsible for their own tax obligations on income earned or payments made through the Platform.

No hidden charges. The exact platform fee and total are shown to the Provider on the job screen before any payment is made.
